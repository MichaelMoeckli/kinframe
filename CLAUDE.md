# Kinframe

## What this project is

Kinframe is a DTC e-commerce concept: customers upload a family photo, an AI pipeline transforms it into a warm, hand-illustrated/painterly portrait (Ghibli-esque aesthetic, but not Ghibli — IP-safe), and they buy it as a framed canvas wall art print, delivered to their door. The wedge is not the AI model — AI generation is a commodity — it is collapsing the entire flow (upload → preview → buy framed product → ship) into one seamless, outcome-focused experience. The customer pays for the keepsake on their wall, not the tool.

## Target customer & positioning

The buyer is a parent or partner aged 30–55, reached primarily through Meta/Instagram ads. Gift occasions (Mother's Day, anniversaries, Christmas, new baby) are the core demand drivers. Price point is $89–129 for a framed canvas, justified by the emotional weight of the keepsake.

Positioning is critical and easy to get wrong. This is **not** marketed as "AI art" — that framing scares the audience and feels cheap. The brand voice is "hand-illustrated family heirloom" or "storybook portrait": warm, artisanal, gift-feeling. The aesthetic is Ghibli-adjacent (soft light, painterly warmth, dreamy illustration) without ever invoking Ghibli names, characters, or imagery.

## Business model

Roughly: $89–129 sale price, ~$0.50 AI generation cost, ~$25 POD fulfillment (Printful or Gelato), ~$25–40 Meta CAC, leaving ~$25–40 gross margin per first-order unit. Margin improves through upsells (multiple sizes, additional prints, additional family members) and repeat purchases (annual portraits, seasonal variants, different styles). The growth engine is performance marketing on Meta — the ad creative (scroll-stopping before/after of a real photo morphing into a framed wall version) is the moat.

## What this project is NOT

It is not a generic AI image generator. It is not a print-on-demand marketplace. It is not competing on features with Photoleap or Printify. It is a focused premium brand around one specific emotional purchase, sold to people who would never use an AI tool themselves.

## How to help on this project

When working on anything Kinframe-related — site copy, ad creative, product decisions, pricing, fulfillment partner choices, brand voice, feature scope — use the positioning above as the baseline. Avoid suggesting things that conflict with it: do not lean into AI branding, do not add features that complicate the seamless flow, do not drift into low-price or novelty territory, do not propose adjacent SKUs (mugs, blankets, pet portraits) before the core canvas product is proven.
